# TUSKSHIELD (ඇත්පවුර) — ANTIGRAVITY AGENT RULES

1. STRICT FOLDER OWNERSHIP (ZERO GIT CONFLICTS):

   - Dev B (Friend) agents ONLY edit: lib/services/, supabase/, & lib/features_admin/
   - LOCKED FILES: Never edit pubspec.yaml, lib/core/, or lib/models/ without a joint Mon/Tue/Wed 9:30 AM sync.

2. DESIGN SYSTEM CONTRACT:
   - Primary: Jungle Green (#2D6A4F) | Alert: Safety Amber (#F59E0B)
   - Danger: Crimson (#EF4444) | Charity: Soft Gold (#D97706)
   - Background: Warm Earth (#F4F1EA) | Driver SOS: Deep Slate (#0F172A)
   - Typography: DM Sans (English), Noto Sans Sinhala, Noto Sans Tamil.

3. ZERO-COST & RURAL RESILIENCE:
   - Compress all photos via flutter_image_compress to 1080p WebP (75% quality, ~120KB) BEFORE upload or Hive outbox storage.
   - Use flutter_map (OpenStreetMap) + PostGIS ST_DWithin (50m road snap).
   - Auto-expire queued offline road-crossing reports older than 2 hours.

5 recommendations to keep TuskShield on track

  • 1. The "Wednesday 1:30 PM Handoff" Ritual

    Because you are unavailable Thursday & Friday while your friend works 9:30 AM–2:00 PM, spend 15 minutes every Wednesday at 1:30 PM agreeing on the exact mock interfaces so neither of you is blocked for the next 4 days.

  • 2. Mock-First Repository Pattern in Week 2

    Have your friend create MockIncidentService returning dummy elephant sightings on Day 1 of Week 2. You can build and test all 18 Flutter UI screens immediately without waiting for PostGIS queries to finish.

  • 3. Pre-Download Sri Lanka Highway Geometry Only

    Instead of importing every footpath in Sri Lanka into Supabase (which hits the 500MB free PostGIS cap), filter OpenStreetMap via Overpass to import only highway=primary|secondary|tertiary|trunk in HEC zones (Habarana, Anuradhapura, Polonnaruwa, Monaragala, Hambantota) — takes under 18 MB!

  • 4. Use One Shared Supabase Project for Both Apps

    Both the User App and TuskShield Admin App connect to the exact same Supabase URL & Anon Key, differentiated only by an is_admin role flag in PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS).

  • 5. Seed 15 Conservation Stories Before Launch

    To avoid an empty app on launch day, pre-populate the Community tab in Week 8 with 15 real Sri Lankan elephant identification guides, first-aid tips, and safe-driving infographics.
